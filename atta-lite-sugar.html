About Atta Sugar-Lite

Is this actually sugar-free?

It carries the "No Added Sugar" claim, not "Sugar Free". A small amount of natural sugar remains from the milk solids. It is sweetened with maltitol, a polyol, instead of added sugar.

How long do they stay fresh?

Best before 90 days from packing. Keep the box closed in a cool, dry place once opened.

Are they vegetarian? Do they contain egg?

100% vegetarian. No egg. They contain milk and wheat (gluten).
