Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Munchingo ("we", "us", "our") is a sole proprietorship of Prashant Nahata, operating from H. No. 43, Sector 14, Faridabad, Haryana, India. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you interact with us — through this website, WhatsApp, or Instagram — and how we use it.

Information we collect

We collect only what's needed to take and fulfil an order, or to respond to your query:

  • Contact details — your name, phone number (WhatsApp), and email address (only if you choose to give it, for order-confirmation emails).
  • Delivery address — collected when you place an order, used solely for shipping.
  • Order details — items, quantities, and amount paid.
  • Payment information — we never see or store your card, UPI, or bank details. Payments are processed directly by Razorpay, a licensed payment gateway, under their own privacy policy.
  • WhatsApp messages — if you message us on WhatsApp, that conversation is handled through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy.
  • Basic site analytics — we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is privacy-first: it does not use cookies or collect any personally identifiable information, and only reports aggregate traffic (e.g. page views).

How we use your information

  • To confirm, process, and ship your order.
  • To send order-status updates and payment links, on WhatsApp and (if you provided one) by email.
  • To respond to questions or support requests you send us.
  • To improve our products and this website.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone. We do not run third-party ad-tracking on this site.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers required to fulfil your order: Razorpay (payments), our courier partner (delivery), Meta/WhatsApp (order communication), Resend (transactional email), and Supabase (secure database hosting for order records). Each of these processes data under their own privacy terms. We do not share your information with anyone else, and we never sell it.

How long we keep it

We retain order records for as long as needed for accounting, warranty, and legal record-keeping purposes (typically up to 7 years, in line with standard Indian business record-keeping practice), after which they may be deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by writing to info.munchingo@gmail.com. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

Children's privacy

Munchingo is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our practices or the law change. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the latest version.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at info.munchingo@gmail.com or on WhatsApp.
