FAQ

Corporate gifting, answered

What is the minimum for corporate orders?

Corporate and bulk orders start at 25 boxes. For smaller quantities, the gift sets on our gift builder can be ordered online.

Can I choose the flavours?

Yes. Tell us the quantity and flavours on WhatsApp and we will put a box together. The base options are the Trio (any three flavours), the Full Range (all four), or single boxes.

How do I pay?

All orders are prepaid. We share the final quote and a secure payment link once the details are agreed.

Where is it made, and what is on the label?

Munchingo cookies are baked by Krazy Bakers, our baking partner in Gangashahar, Bikaner, in small batches. Whole wheat atta, pure desi ghee, no maida and no palm oil. Our FSSAI licence number and full ingredient lists are on our about page and each product page.

Is there an allergen I should tell my recipients about?

Our cookies contain wheat (gluten) and milk, and are made in a facility that also processes tree nuts and other spices. Atta Sugar-Lite is sweetened with maltitol, a polyol, which can have a laxative effect in excess. It is not a medical product.

Do you give a GST invoice?

We are GST-registered (GSTIN 06AIIPN5005C2ZP). Tell us your company details when you ask for a quote.
