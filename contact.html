Is Atta Lite-sugar safe for diabetics?

Atta Lite-sugar has no added sugar and is sweetened with maltitol — 95% less sugar than our Original. It is not a medical product and we make no health claims. Please note: contains polyols; excess consumption may have a laxative effect.

What's the shelf life?

Best before 90 days from packing, stored in a dry and cool place. We bake in small batches and ship within 2 days from Bikaner, so what you receive is always fresh.

Yes — corporate gifting and bulk orders from 25 boxes. Tell us the quantity and flavours on WhatsApp and we'll put a box together.

Where is it made?

Krazy Baker, our bakery in Gangashahar, Bikaner, Rajasthan — perfecting whole wheat atta and pure ghee baking for over a decade.
