What makes it Ajwain

Real ajwain. Nothing pretending.

Whole carom seed, mixed into every batch with pure desi ghee and just enough salt to round it out. Less sweet than our other flavours, though not sugar-free (see the nutrition table).

Ingredients

Whole wheat atta (49.9%), desi cow ghee, sugar, milk solids (milk powder), glucose, salt, raising agent (INS 503(ii)), ajwain (carom seed).

Contains: wheat (gluten), milk. Made in a facility that also processes tree nuts and other spices.

Best before 90 days from packing. Store in a cool, dry place. 100% vegetarian.

Questions

About Atta Ajwain

Is Ajwain very spicy?

No, it is warm and aromatic rather than hot: whole carom seed and salt, no chilli. It is less sweet than our other flavours, but it does contain sugar (19 g per 100 g).

How long do they stay fresh?

Best before 90 days from packing. Keep the box closed in a cool, dry place once opened.

Are they vegetarian? Do they contain egg?

100% vegetarian. No egg. They contain milk and wheat (gluten).
