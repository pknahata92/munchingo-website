About Us

Made With Intent.

Whole wheat atta at the top of the ingredient list, because atta is actually the biggest thing in it. Desi ghee, never palm oil. Real sugar, in honest amounts — not chemistry dressed up as sweetness.

Munchingo is baked by a family-run bakery in Bikaner — a decade-old unit that was perfecting this craft long before Munchingo existed as a brand. We didn't build a factory. We found a family that already knew how to do this properly, and asked them to keep doing exactly that.

"Nothing in this box is pretending to be something it isn't."
