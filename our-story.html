Our story

Why we started Munchingo.

We grew up on atta biscuits. Then we turned the pack over and read the label.

By Prashant Nahata, Munchingo · 12 October 2026

Since childhood, we have been fed handmade atta biscuits by our grandmothers, our parents, our grandfathers and the people around us. Back in our hometown, Bikaner, a good biscuit was simply part of an ordinary day: with tea, with a guest, with nothing in particular.

Then we read the back of the pack

It was only recently that we started turning packs over and reading the ingredient list, and getting to know what actually goes inside the biscuits we buy. A lot of what we found was not what we remembered eating: refined flour where there should be atta, vegetable oil where there should be ghee, and a long tail of additives we could not picture in a kitchen.

That is where Munchingo was born.

Tasty first, better for you underneath

Munchingo does not stand for "healthy". It stands for tasty, indulgent cookies that are simply made better. Ours are made with whole wheat atta and pure desi ghee, with no maida, no palm oil and no preservatives. Nothing in the list should make you feel you are putting something bad into your gut, and you can check that yourself, because every ingredient is printed on the pack and on each product page. Our label guide shows you how to read any biscuit pack, ours included.

Clean food should be the basic, not a luxury

Finding clean, honestly made food has become a kind of luxury, and it should be the bare minimum. We are not inventing anything new here. We are trying to get back to the way this food used to be made, and to a society where reading the label and liking what you see is normal.

Baked in Bikaner

Our cookies are baked in Bikaner by Krazy Bakers and delivered to your doorstep across India. There are four, each for a different moment: Original, Kesari, Ajwain and Sugar-Lite.
